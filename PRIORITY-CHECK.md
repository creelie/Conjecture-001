# Priority check: complements of maximal sublattices of finite semidistributive lattices

Question: had anyone answered the question of Adams, Freese, Nation and Schmid (J. Austral. Math.
Soc. Ser. A 63 (1997), after Theorem 7: "It is natural to ask whether this characterization extends
to finite semidistributive lattices. We do not know."), or proved or refuted Hypothesis 1 of
Adaricheva, Mata, Silberger and Zamojska-Dzienio (arXiv:2507.22682; DMTCS 28:2 (2026),
doi:10.46298/dmtcs.16374)?

Conclusion: as of 13 October 2026 we found no answer to the question and no proof or refutation of
Hypothesis 1. Adaricheva et al. proved it under extra hypotheses (their Theorem 5.5: if some element
of the complement is comparable to every element of the complement, the complement is an interval)
and for convex geometries of convex dimension two; the author's earlier exhaustive check covers all
semidistributive lattices with at most fourteen elements. The counterexample here has 26 elements.
